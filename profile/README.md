# Data Torturing Solutions

We build data-driven web properties for the UK: provenance-tracked local
business directories, reference and guide sites, and the platform and
monitoring tooling that operates them.

## What we run

- **Local business directories** — compiled from public registers (Companies
  House, the ICO register, council rating lists), with every fact carrying
  its source, and published only when it clears explainable quality
  thresholds. Makers Map (Southampton creative businesses) and Wessex Tech
  (BCP-area technology firms) are built this way.
- **Reference and guide sites** — independent, factual sites on subjects from
  UK hill data to degree apprenticeships, each naming its sources.
- **Open engineering documentation** — how the platform works, at
  [data-torturing-solutions.github.io](https://data-torturing-solutions.github.io).

## If you're listed on one of our directories

Every listing page explains where its information came from. From any of our
directory sites you can **claim your listing**, **correct it**, or **have it
removed** — the *Your data* link in the footer is self-service, and removal
is honoured permanently. We are registered with the ICO (registration
**ZC218139**) and every site publishes a full privacy notice naming the
controller.
